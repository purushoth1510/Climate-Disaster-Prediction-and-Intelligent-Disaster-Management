## 🌟 Overview

Climate change has significantly increased the frequency and intensity of natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, droughts, and extreme weather events.

This project utilizes **Machine Learning**, **Climate Data Analysis**, and **Predictive Modeling** to provide:

✅ Early Disaster Prediction

✅ Risk Assessment

✅ Real-Time Monitoring

✅ Intelligent Emergency Management

✅ Data-Driven Decision Support

The system aims to help authorities, organizations, and communities improve disaster preparedness and reduce potential losses. AI-driven disaster prediction systems have become an important area of research for improving disaster resilience and response efficiency. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## 🎯 Objectives

- Predict climate-related disasters using historical environmental data
- Identify high-risk regions and vulnerable areas
- Generate early warning alerts
- Support disaster preparedness planning
- Improve emergency response coordination
- Enhance community resilience

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌧 Flood Prediction
Predict flood-prone situations using rainfall and environmental indicators.

### 🌪 Cyclone Risk Analysis
Analyze weather conditions to estimate cyclone threats.

### 🌡 Climate Trend Monitoring
Monitor environmental changes and abnormal weather patterns.

### 📊 Risk Assessment Dashboard
Visualize disaster probability and risk levels.

### 🚨 Early Warning System
Generate alerts before potential disaster events.

### 🧠 AI-Based Decision Support
Assist disaster management authorities with actionable insights.

### 📍 Region-Based Analysis
Identify vulnerable locations using historical climate records.
